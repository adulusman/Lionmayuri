Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about our services? Check out some common queries below.

How far in advance should I book your services?

We recommend booking our services at least 3-6 months in advance for weddings and large events, and 1-2 months for smaller events. However, we also accommodate last-minute bookings when possible.

What areas do you serve?

We primarily serve Kerala and surrounding areas, but we also handle events across South India and can travel nationally for select events.

Do you offer customized packages?

Yes, we understand that each event is unique. We offer tailored packages to suit your specific requirements and budget.

What is your payment policy?

We typically require a 50% deposit to secure your date, with the remaining balance due one week before the event. We accept various payment methods for your convenience.

Can I meet with you before booking?

Absolutely! We encourage an initial consultation to discuss your vision and requirements. This can be done in person, over the phone, or via video call.
